About this tool

Graph y = a·cos(bx − c) + d with amplitude, period and phase shift sliders. A free online cosine grapher for trigonometry and precalculus.

Cosine looks like sine that has been nudged to the left by a quarter cycle, and that is exactly what it is: cos(x) = sin(x + π/2). Its signature is that it starts at its maximum, 1, when x equals zero. That makes cosine the natural choice for quantities that begin at an extreme, like the height of a Ferris wheel car at the top or the position of a spring pulled to full stretch.

Cosine is also an even function, so its graph is a mirror image across the y-axis, and cos(-x) = cos(x). It crosses the x-axis at odd multiples of π/2 and reaches its minimum at π. To model real situations, combine the standard pieces: 4cos(πx/6) + 10 has a midline of 10, an amplitude of 4 and a period of 12. Use the grapher to check where your model peaks.

  • cos(0) = 1, so the graph starts at its maximum rather than at the origin.
  • Even symmetry: the left half is the mirror of the right half.
  • Cosine equals sine shifted left by π/2, so one formula can be converted into the other.
  • Squares like cos²(x) stay between 0 and 1 and repeat every π.

Solved examples

Amplitude and period

Problem. Analyze y = 2cos(2x) in radian mode.

  1. Amplitude = 2, so the range is -2 to 2.
  2. Period = 2pi/2 = pi.
  3. At x = 0 the cosine starts at its maximum: y = 2. It reaches -2 at x = pi/2.

Answer. Amplitude 2, period pi, y-intercept 2. Graph it in the calculator

Reflection and midline

Problem. Find the range of y = -3cos(x) + 1.

  1. The amplitude is 3 and the midline is y = 1.
  2. Maximum: 1 + 3 = 4. Minimum: 1 - 3 = -2.
  3. The negative sign flips the curve, so it starts at its minimum: y(0) = -3 + 1 = -2.

Answer. Range [-2, 4], period 2pi. Graph it in the calculator

How to use the Cosine Grapher

  • Enter cos(x). Type it and confirm the curve starts at 1 on the y-axis.
  • Compare with sine. Add sin(x) in a second row and see the quarter-cycle gap between the two.
  • Reflect it. Put a minus sign in front to start at the minimum.
  • Build a model. Try 4*cos(pi*x/6) + 10 and read the midline, the maximum and the period.
  • Test even symmetry. Trace at x = 1 and x = -1 and confirm that the heights are equal.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the cosine graph start?

At (0, 1), the maximum. Sine starts at the origin instead, which is the easiest way to tell them apart.

Is cosine really just a shifted sine?

Yes. Shifting sine left by π/2 gives cosine, so either can describe the same wave with a different phase.

Why is cosine an even function?

Because cos(-x) = cos(x). The graph is symmetric about the y-axis, like a parabola.

Where are the zeros of cosine?

At x = π/2, 3π/2 and every odd multiple of π/2, where the curve crosses the axis.

How do I model a cycle that starts at a peak?

Use a cosine with no phase shift, such as A cos(Bx) + D. A sine model would need an extra shift to start at the top.
