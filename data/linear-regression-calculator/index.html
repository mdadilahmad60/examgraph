About this tool

Updated November 2026 · Free, no sign-up

Paste x, y data for the least-squares line, slope, intercept, r and R², with the scatter plot and regression line graphed instantly.

Linear regression finds the straight line y = mx + b that best fits a set of points by minimizing the sum of squared vertical distances (least squares). It is one of the most-used tools in school math and statistics, and it appears on the Digital SAT, AP Precalculus, AP Statistics and state tests.

Paste your data into the box (two columns copied from Google Sheets or Excel work), press Fit & graph, and you get the equation, the correlation coefficient r, R² and a prediction box. The scatter plot and regression line are drawn together, and you can share the graph with a link.

  • Slope m: the change in y for each 1-unit increase in x.
  • Intercept b: the predicted y when x = 0 (it only makes sense if x = 0 is in range).
  • Residual: actual y minus predicted y. A good linear fit has residuals scattered randomly around 0.

To keep practicing, try these related tools: Line of Best Fit & Scatter Plot Maker, Quadratic Regression Calculator and Exponential Regression Calculator.

Solved examples

Fit a line to four points

Problem. Find the least-squares line for (1, 2), (2, 4.1), (3, 5.9), (4, 8.2) and the correlation coefficient.

  1. With n = 4: Σx = 10, Σy = 20.2, Σxy = 60.7 and Σx² = 30.
  2. Slope m = (4·60.7 − 10·20.2)/(4·30 − 10²) = 40.8/20 = 2.04.
  3. Intercept b = (20.2 − 2.04·10)/4 = −0.05.
  4. The correlation coefficient is r ≈ 0.999, so the points are almost perfectly linear.

Answer. y = 2.04x − 0.05 with r ≈ 0.999. Paste the points in the panel and choose Linear to see the same result. Graph it in the calculator

Predict and find a residual

Problem. Use y = 2.04x − 0.05 to predict y at x = 5, and find the residual at x = 3.

  1. Prediction: y = 2.04(5) − 0.05 = 10.15.
  2. At x = 3 the line gives 2.04(3) − 0.05 = 6.07, while the data value is 5.9.
  3. Residual = actual − predicted = 5.9 − 6.07 = −0.17.

Answer. Predict 10.15 at x = 5. The residual at x = 3 is −0.17, so the point lies just below the line. x = 5 is outside the data range, so treat it as an extrapolation. Graph it in the calculator

How to use the Linear Regression Calculator

  • Type an equation. Write it in terms of x, for example x^2 - 4 or 2x + 1. Circles, ellipses and inequalities work too: try x^2 + y^2 = 9 or y > 2x + 1 (shaded). On a phone, tap the equation box and use the built-in math keyboard for powers, roots, fractions and trig.
  • Compare curves. Tap + to add more equations. Intersections, roots, intercepts and turning points are marked automatically, and you can tap any entry under Key points to jump to it.
  • Use sliders. Any extra letter becomes a slider, so a*x^2 + b*x + c lets you drag a, b and c.
  • Move around. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, or use the + / − buttons. The home button resets the view and the corner button opens a full-screen graph.
  • Plot points and fit a regression. Type a point such as (2, 5) or a list like (1, 2), (2, 4), (3, 5). For real data, open Data table & regression, paste x, y pairs and pick linear, quadratic, cubic, exponential, power or logarithmic: the best-fit equation, R² and a prediction box appear instantly.
  • Degrees or radians. Tap RAD / DEG beside the equations to switch angle mode.
  • Name, share or save. Edit the graph title above the plot. Settings lets you download a PNG or JPG, or open a print-ready page to save as PDF. The share button copies a link that reopens your equations and graph title.
  • Practice for tests. Turn on US Exam Mode in Settings to lock key-point hints and tooltips, like a real testing calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What do r and R² mean?

r (from -1 to 1) measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship. R² (from 0 to 1) is the share of the variation in y explained by the line. An R² of 0.95 means the line explains 95% of the variation.

How do I paste data from a spreadsheet?

Select two columns (x then y) in Google Sheets or Excel, copy, and paste into the box. Tabs, commas, spaces and semicolons all work as separators.

Can I predict a value?

Yes. After fitting, type an x value into Predict y at x and the estimate appears immediately.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing you paste is sent to a server.
