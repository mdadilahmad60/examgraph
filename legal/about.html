About ExamGrapher

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ExamGrapher.com is a free, web-based online graphing calculator built for US high school, AP and college mathematics students. It opens instantly in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, Chromebook or computer, with no download, no account and no payment. You type an equation, and the graph appears.

We started ExamGrapher because students spend a lot of time practicing for tests such as the Digital SAT, the PSAT, the ACT, AP Precalculus, AP Calculus and state exams, and many of them do not own a handheld graphing calculator. A good graphing tool should be available to everyone, so we built one that is fast, clear and free.

Who ExamGrapher is for

Our main audience is students in the United States, from Algebra 1 through college calculus and statistics. Teachers, tutors and parents also use the site to demonstrate ideas in class, to prepare worked examples and to check homework. Because every calculator is a separate page with its own explanation, it is easy to find the right tool for a topic and share a link with a class.

What you can do with ExamGrapher

The site has more than 60 calculators and graphers. Each one runs in your browser and shares the same graphing engine, so what you learn on one page works on the others.

  • Plot several equations at once, including lines, parabolas, circles, inequalities, piecewise functions, polar curves and parametric curves.
  • See roots, intercepts, turning points and intersections marked automatically, and tap them to jump to the exact coordinates.
  • Drag sliders for any extra letter in your equation, such as a, b and c in a*x^2 + b*x + c, to see how each parameter changes the curve.
  • Paste x, y data and fit linear, quadratic, cubic, exponential, power or logarithmic regressions, with R-squared and predictions.
  • Switch between radians and degrees, and turn on US Exam Mode to practice without hints or key-point labels, like a real test calculator.
  • Explore calculus topics such as derivatives, integrals, tangent lines, slope fields and Taylor polynomials, and plot 3D surfaces.
  • Use statistics calculators for z-scores, standard deviation, confidence intervals, t-tests and chi-square tests.
  • Save a graph as a PNG or JPG, print it to PDF, or copy a link that reopens your equations.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: make graphing and data tools clear, accurate and accessible to every student, whatever device they have. We want a student with only a school Chromebook to practice with the same quality of tools as a student who owns a handheld calculator. We also want teachers to be able to open a page and teach, without logins or installation.

Why ExamGrapher is free

ExamGrapher is free to use and does not require sign-up. The site is a lean static website, which keeps running costs low, and that lets us offer every calculator at no charge. To help cover hosting and development, we may show non-intrusive advertising in the future. If we do, it will never block the calculator, and it will follow the choices you make in our cookie settings. You can read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.

How we keep it accurate and private

Calculations run in your browser using our own graphing engine, which is covered by automated tests that check hundreds of equations and the statistics formulas against reference values. Your equations and graphs stay on your device unless you choose to share a link. We collect only the information described in our Privacy Policy, such as an email address if you join the cheat sheet list or send us a message.

A note about exams and trademarks

ExamGrapher is an independent practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the College Board, ACT, Inc., Desmos, Texas Instruments or any state testing agency. Exam rules about calculators change, so always check the current policy for your test before relying on any tool. Read more in our Terms of Use.

What is coming next

We are always improving the calculators. Our plans include more worked examples and guides for each tool, additional exam-prep pages for more state tests, more statistics and data tools, richer 3D graphing, an improved workspace for notes and tables, and more languages. Student and teacher feedback decides what we build first.

Get in touch

We read every message. If you found a bug, have a feature idea, want to suggest a topic or need help, visit our Contact page or message us on WhatsApp or email.
