Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ExamGrapher ("ExamGrapher", "we", "us" or "our") collects when you use examgraph.netlify.app and its graphing calculators, how we use it, who we share it with and what choices you have. We designed the site to collect as little personal information as possible. Using the calculators does not require an account, and your equations stay in your browser.

By using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site, or contact us with your concerns using the details at the end of this page.

1. Who we are

ExamGrapher is a free online graphing calculator and math tool website operated from India. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at adilwebsolutions1@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 82 1022 5989. See our Contact page for more options.

2. Information we collect

We collect three kinds of information.

  • Information you give us. If you join the cheat sheet list, we receive your name, your email address, whether you identify as a student or teacher, and the page you signed up from. If you use our contact form, email or WhatsApp, we receive your name, email address or phone number, and the content of your message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical data when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages requested and the date and time. We use this for security, reliability and basic traffic measurement.
  • Information stored on your device. The calculator stores small items in your browser, for example your theme choice, graph titles and your cookie choices, so the site works the way you left it. These items stay on your device. See the Cookies section below.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information. The equations, data tables, notes and graphs you create are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. If you copy a share link, your equations are contained in the link itself, so only share it with people you trust.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the calculators, pages and features.
  • To send the cheat sheet you requested and, if you chose to join the list, an occasional email about new tools. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To reply to your messages, fix bugs and provide support.
  • To protect the site against spam, abuse and security threats, including through hidden honeypot fields on our forms.
  • To understand in aggregate how the site is used, only where analytics is enabled and you have agreed to it.
  • To show advertising, only if ads are enabled on the site and you have agreed where the law requires consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Use.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following bases. We process email sign-ups and optional cookies based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. We process messages you send us to take steps you ask for and for our legitimate interest in supporting users. We process server logs and security data for our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure and working. Where the law requires, we process data to meet legal obligations.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files placed on your device. We also use similar technologies such as browser local storage and caches. We use them in the following categories. Items marked as not currently active are not in use today; if we turn them on in the future, they will only run if you give consent.

Essential items are needed for the site to work as you expect and do not require consent. You can accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose by category using the cookie banner. To change your mind at any time, use the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page. You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings; this will reset your theme and graph titles.

7. Third-party services

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify, which hosts the site, delivers it through a content network and processes submissions from our forms. Netlify may process IP addresses and request logs. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.
  • Email and messaging providers, such as Gmail and WhatsApp (operated by Meta), if you contact us through those channels. Their own privacy policies apply to messages you send them.
  • Advertising and analytics providers. We may add services such as Google AdSense or Google Analytics in the future. If we do, we will update this policy, list them here, and load them only according to your cookie choices.

8. Advertising and analytics

If we show ads, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Where required, ads that use cookies or personal data will only load if you accept advertising cookies. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or at aboutads.info, and you can change your choice on our site at any time through Cookie settings. If analytics is enabled, we will use it to understand which tools are useful, not to identify you personally.

9. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising unless you have opted in to advertising cookies. We share information only with the service providers described above, when required by law or valid legal process, to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the site, or in connection with a business transfer, in which case we will tell you if your data becomes subject to a different policy.

10. Data retention

We keep email sign-ups until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless the law requires longer. Hosting logs are kept by our provider for a limited period according to their policy. Data stored in your browser stays until you delete it.

11. International data transfers

We operate from India, and our hosting provider serves the site from servers in several countries, including the United States. Your information may therefore be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses used by our providers.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights. These include rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, other US state privacy laws, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

  • Access: ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to fix information that is wrong.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your information, including removal from the email list.
  • Withdraw consent: change your cookie choices at any time, and unsubscribe from emails using the link in each message.
  • Portability and restriction or objection to processing, where the law provides them.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: we do not sell personal information. You can also use the Reject option in the cookie banner.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for using these rights.
  • Complaint: you may complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any right, email adilwebsolutions1@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp, and tell us what you would like us to do. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days.

13. Children's privacy

ExamGrapher is made for students, and many of our visitors are teenagers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The calculators work without giving us any personal information, and our email forms are meant for people aged 13 and over, or for a parent or teacher acting for a child. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), security headers and a hosting provider with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as exam boards or other tools. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add features or services. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make material changes, we will highlight them on the site, and where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.

17. Contact us

If you have questions, requests or concerns about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your data, contact us by email at adilwebsolutions1@gmail.com, on WhatsApp at +91 82 1022 5989, or through the form on our Contact page.
