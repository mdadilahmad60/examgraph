Calculus & 3D Graphing Calculators

Calculus is a visual subject. Use these tools to graph a function with its derivative, shade the area under a curve, draw a tangent line or explore a 3D surface.

They are built for AP Calculus and college courses, and every calculator works on a phone, tablet or computer without an account.

All 8 tools in this category

3D Graphing CalculatorPlot 3D surfaces z = f(x, y) and implicit equations like x² + y² + z² = 9 online. Rotate, zoom and read a colour legend, free in your browser.Parametric & Lissajous Curve GrapherGraph parametric equations x(t), y(t) and Lissajous curves online. A free parametric grapher with sliders to explore circles, spirals and loops.Polar Graphing CalculatorGraph polar equations such as roses, cardioids and spirals in your browser. A free polar graphing calculator with degree and radian modes.Derivative Graphing CalculatorGraph a function and its derivative with d/dx( ). See slopes, maxima and minima line up, then check the second derivative too. Free online.Integral Graphing CalculatorShade the area under a curve with int(f, a, b) and see the value. Also graph the accumulation function. Free online integral grapher for calculus.Tangent Line GrapherGraph a curve together with its tangent line at any point. A free online tangent line grapher to see slope and linear approximation clearly.Slope Field & Solution Curve GrapherDraw a slope field for dy/dx = f(x, y) and a solution curve through any starting point. Free online slope field grapher for AP Calculus BC.Taylor Polynomial Approximation GrapherCompare sin(x) or eˣ with Taylor polynomial approximations on one graph. A free online Taylor series grapher for AP Calculus BC and college.
