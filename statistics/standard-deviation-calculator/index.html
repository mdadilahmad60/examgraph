Standard Deviation Calculator

About this calculator

Updated November 2026 · Free, runs in your browser

Standard deviation measures how spread out numbers are around their mean. A small value means the data sits close to the average, and a large value means it is more spread out. Paste your numbers and this calculator returns the sample and population versions, plus variance, mean, median, range, standard error and a step-by-step table of deviations.

Sample or population?

Use population standard deviation (divide by n) when your numbers are the whole group you care about, such as every score in a class of 25. Use sample standard deviation (divide by n − 1) when your numbers are a sample from a larger group and you want to estimate that group's spread. Dividing by n − 1 is called Bessel's correction. Most statistics courses expect the sample formula unless the problem says the data is a population.

Formulas

  • Mean: x̄ = Σx / n
  • Sample variance: s² = Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)
  • Population variance: σ² = Σ(x − μ)² / N
  • Standard deviation is the square root of the variance.
  • Standard error of the mean: s / √n

Worked example

For 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 7, 9, 5 the mean is 47 / 8 = 5.875 and the sum of squared deviations is 28.875. The sample variance is 28.875 / 7 = 4.125, so the sample standard deviation is about 2.031. The population variance is 28.875 / 8 = 3.609, so the population standard deviation is about 1.900. The calculator opens with this data.

What a standard deviation tells you

Standard deviation is in the same units as your data, unlike variance, which is in squared units. In a roughly normal data set about 68% of values fall within one standard deviation of the mean and about 95% within two. Because every value is squared in the calculation, outliers have a large effect, so check for extreme values.

Practice problem

Find the standard deviation of 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 7. The mean is 33/6 = 5.5. The squared deviations are 2.25, 6.25, 0.25, 0.25, 6.25 and 2.25, which add to 17.5.

Sample variance = 17.5/5 = 3.5, so the sample standard deviation is about 1.871. Population variance = 17.5/6, about 2.917, so the population standard deviation is about 1.708. Paste the six numbers into the calculator to confirm both.

How one outlier changes the answer

Add the value 40 to the list above. The mean jumps from 5.5 to about 10.43 and the sample standard deviation from about 1.87 to about 13.15. A single extreme value can multiply the spread, which is why standard deviation is paired with a plot or the median and quartiles when data may contain outliers.

Common mistakes

  • Dividing by n when the data is a sample. Use n - 1 for a sample.
  • Forgetting to take the square root, which gives the variance instead.
  • Mixing units. The standard deviation has the same units as the data; the variance has squared units.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between variance and standard deviation?

Variance is the average squared distance from the mean. Standard deviation is its square root, which puts the spread back into the original units of the data.

Can standard deviation be negative?

No. It is a square root, so it is zero or positive. It is zero only when every value in the data set is identical.

How many numbers do I need?

At least 2 for a sample standard deviation, because the formula divides by n − 1. Larger samples give more reliable estimates.

What is the difference between standard deviation and standard error?

Standard deviation describes how spread out individual values are. Standard error of the mean (s divided by the square root of n) describes how much the sample mean would vary from sample to sample.

How do outliers affect the result?

A single extreme value can increase the standard deviation a lot. If your data has outliers, also look at the median and the range, or use a measure such as the interquartile range.

Can I paste data from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Copy a column from Excel or Google Sheets and paste it in. Numbers can be separated by commas, spaces or new lines, and negative numbers and decimals work.
